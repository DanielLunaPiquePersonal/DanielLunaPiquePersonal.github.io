About Me

I am a Gameplay and Systems Programmer with hands-on studio experience in R&D and core game development at Flynn's Arcade. My focus lies in building robust software architectures, implementing gameplay mechanics, and solving complex technical challenges. I excel at debugging and optimization, ensuring that the core foundation of a game runs smoothly and efficiently.

Beyond technical execution, I have a deep passion for Game Design and Narrative. While my primary role is writing clean, scalable code, I excel at adding "soul" to a project. I love bridging the gap between mechanics and storytelling—whether that means implementing environmental cues, fine-tuning game feel, or seamlessly integrating custom music and voice acting performances to elevate the atmosphere. I thrive in multidisciplinary environments, believing that the best technical solutions are those that directly serve the game's creative and emotional vision.
